IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Privacy, Data Use and Trading Disclaimer

Eagle Smart Scanner is designed as a research and decision-support dashboard. It does not guarantee profit, future returns or the accuracy of any market prediction.

1. Research Tool Only

BUY and STRONG BUY signals are calculated from verified technical, fundamental, sector and chart-pattern data. These signals are research outputs and not personalized investment advice.

2. No Profit Guarantee

Market prices may move against the signal. Probability, target and holding-period values are estimates based on available data and should never be treated as guaranteed outcomes.

3. Risk Management

Always verify entry, stop-loss, target, position size, liquidity and current market conditions before placing any trade or investment order.

4. Data Sources

Live and historical market data may be obtained through FYERS. Fundamental data may be obtained through a configured third-party provider such as Financial Modeling Prep.

5. Missing Data

The dashboard does not intentionally create fake prices, fake financial values or artificial BUY signals. Missing, stale or invalid data may cause a stock to be rejected or displayed as NO TRADE.

6. Authentication

FYERS authentication is completed through the official FYERS login process. The dashboard does not ask you to enter your FYERS password directly.

7. Access Tokens

The application may temporarily store an access token in the server session or in-memory cache so that live market data can be requested. Tokens should never be committed to GitHub or written directly inside source-code files.

8. Third-Party Services

Availability and accuracy depend on external APIs, internet connectivity, exchange data and provider limits. The dashboard may become temporarily unavailable when a provider is down or a rate limit is reached.

9. Background Scanning

Scans may continue in the background and cached results may be shown for faster loading. Results marked stale or unverified should not be used for trading decisions.

10. User Responsibility

You remain fully responsible for every trade, investment, order, loss, tax consequence and financial decision made using information from this dashboard.

Final warning: Trading and investing involve financial risk. Never invest money that you cannot afford to lose, and consult a qualified financial professional when required.
